### 🍃 About Me

Hello, I'm **Vile**!

I'm a creative developer and digital artist with a love for horses, fantasy worlds, artwork and all things cutesy.

I spend my time creating custom equine coats, designing artwork, experimenting with Minecraft scripting and building welcoming little communities.

My biggest passion project is **Official SafeHaven**, a cozy, forest-inspired Minecraft community built around horses, animals, creativity and kindness.

### 🌳 Official SafeHaven

*Under the Hope Tree, there's always room for one more.*

Official SafeHaven is my Minecraft community, where players can create homesteads, care for horses and livestock, explore and make memories.

- 🐎 Star Worm Equestrian Mod (SWEM)
- 🌿 Cottagecore and fantasy-inspired gameplay
- 🐑 Animal care, breeding and genetics
- 🏡 Community villages and homesteads
- ✨ Custom KubeJS scripts and features
- 🍄 Quests, events and community activities
